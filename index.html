Privacy Policy

Application: Football Points Table (Android)      Effective date: October 10, 2026      Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Football Points Table (the “App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The App is developed and published by Faizan Nauman (the “Developer”). By installing or using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App and uninstall it.

2. Summary in Plain Language

  • Tournament data (teams, matches, scores) that you enter is stored locally on your device.
  • The App displays third-party advertisements (such as Google AdMob) to support development.
  • There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login required.
  • Third-party ad networks may collect device identifiers and diagnostic information to serve and personalize ads.
  • Nothing you enter in tournaments leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it.
  • You can delete your data at any time, and uninstalling the App removes all local data.

3. Data Collection & Third-Party Advertisements (Google AdMob)

While tournament data (teams, players, matches, and scores) remains strictly on your device, the App integrates Google AdMob as a third-party advertising network to display ads. To serve, manage, and personalize advertisements, third-party ad networks and SDKs may automatically collect certain technical information when you use the App, including:

  • Device Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or similar device advertising identifiers.
  • Network Information: IP address and general network connection details.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: App crash logs, diagnostic info, and interaction data used for ad measurement and fraud prevention.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our app, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Information You Enter on Your Device

To function, the App stores the content you create: tournament names, team names, player names, match dates, seasons, scores, goal scorers, assist providers, point rules, and your theme choice. This content is saved in the App's private storage on your device (Android SharedPreferences, as JSON text). We never receive a copy of your tournament data.

5. How the Information Is Used

Information stored locally in the App is used only on your device to calculate standings, show statistics, and produce exports you request. Technical data collected by AdMob is used solely for serving, measuring, and personalizing advertisements in accordance with Google Play policies.

6. Sharing and Exports (Your Choice)

The App lets you export or share content. These actions happen only when you initiate them:

  • PNG images are saved to your device's Gallery (through Android MediaStore).
  • PDF reports are created on your device and handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet (through Android FileProvider).
  • Text and JSON can be copied to the clipboard or shared with another app.

Once you send something to another app, person, or service, that app's or recipient's privacy practices apply. We do not control what you share.

7. Third-Party Services and Libraries

The App integrates Google AdMob for monetization. Additionally, it uses open-source Android libraries (AndroidX, Material Components, Kotlin). Google Play and your device's operating system may also collect installation and diagnostic information under Google's and your device manufacturer's privacy policies.

8. Data Storage and Security

Your local tournament data is kept in the App's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Third-party ad data collection is handled securely by Google AdMob over encrypted connections. You are responsible for your device's security; we recommend using a screen lock and keeping Android updated.

9. Backup and Device Transfer

Depending on your device settings, Android may include the App's data in a Google account backup or device-to-device transfer. Those backups are handled by Google or your device manufacturer under their own policies.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies Policy

Application: Football Points Table (Android)      Effective date: October 10, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device to remember preferences or track activity. Similar technologies include advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), web beacons, and local storage.

2. Overview

While the core tournament features of Football Points Table store data exclusively on your device without tracking, the app utilizes third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) that employ advertising identifiers and cookies for ad delivery and measurement.

3. Local Storage Used by the App

The App saves your own tournament content locally on your device in private storage (SharedPreferences) for functionality only.

4. Third-Party Advertising IDs, Cookies, and Trackers (Google AdMob)

Google AdMob uses advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), cookies, and similar technologies to support ad measurement, frequency capping, anti-fraud protection, and personalized ad delivery. These tools collect technical data about your device and app usage to provide relevant advertisements. Users can manage or reset their advertising identifier and opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android Settings: navigate to Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads depending on Android version), where you can reset your advertising ID or delete it.

5. Your Choices

You can control ad personalization and advertising identifiers directly in your Android device settings:

  • Go to Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Tap Reset advertising ID to clear existing advertising data associations.
  • Tap Delete advertising ID if you wish to remove your advertising identifier.

To remove all locally stored app data, go to Android Settings → Apps → Football Points Table → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App.

6. Changes and Contact

For questions regarding cookies and tracking technologies, please contact us at faizan.dev.support@gmail.com.
